Good morning everyone. Today I'll be presenting the results of our final-year project on multimodal presentation assessment. Our goal was simple to state but harder to solve: can a machine learning system meaningfully evaluate how well someone delivers a presentation, using both what they say and how they say it? We combined speech analysis with visual behavior tracking, and processed both through temporal deep learning models to capture how a presentation changes over time, not just how it looks in any single frame. In the next few minutes I'll walk through our architecture, our evaluation results, and what we'd do differently with more time.
