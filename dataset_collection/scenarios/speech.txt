Distinguished guests, faculty, and fellow students, thank you for the opportunity to stand before you today. When we began this project, we were told that presentation skill was something you either had or didn't — something intangible, hard to teach, and even harder to measure. We disagreed. Over the past year, we built a system that treats presentation delivery the way we'd treat any other measurable skill: broken into components, tracked over time, and improved through deliberate, specific feedback. What I want to share with you today is not just what we built, but why we believe measurable feedback changes how people practice — and how people improve.
